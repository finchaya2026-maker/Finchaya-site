What this site is

What the figures mean, and what they do not

The holdings shown are each fund's most recently disclosed portfolio. Two consequences follow, and both matter:

  • Portfolios are disclosed monthly, so what you see is what the fund held at the disclosure date — not necessarily what it holds today.
  • Every figure on this site describes what has already happened. None of it is a forecast. Past performance may or may not be sustained in future.

Where a category's funds are not genuine peers, no category placing is shown.

Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short. You can cancel any time from the Subscription button on the site. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again. We do not refund a period that has already started, except where we charged you in error.

Plans and prices

FinChaya offers a paid subscription in two plans: monthly (₹149 a month) and yearly (₹999 a year). Prices are in Indian rupees. The price shown at checkout is the amount charged.

Automatic renewal

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, using the payment method you authorised at checkout, until you cancel. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Your bank or card issuer may notify you before each charge. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

How to cancel

  • Sign in, press Subscription at the top of the page, and choose Cancel subscription.
  • Cancelling stops all future charges. Access to paid features continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • If you cannot sign in, email finchaya2026@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will cancel it for you.

Refunds

  • Time already paid for is not refunded. If you cancel part-way through a month or a year, you keep access to the end of that period but we do not return money for the unused part.
  • Charged in error, or charged twice: if we billed you the wrong amount, or more than once for the same period, we will refund the extra charge in full. Please email us within 30 days of the charge.
  • Charged after cancelling: if you cancelled before the renewal date and were still charged, we will refund that charge in full.

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show them.

If a payment fails

If a renewal payment does not go through, Razorpay retries it over the following days. Your access continues for a few days past the end of the paid period while that happens. If the payment still fails, the subscription stops and access ends. Nothing further is charged.

Changes to price

If we change the price, we will tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies to you, and you can cancel before then.

Contact

Questions about a charge or a cancellation: finchaya2026@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 3 working days.

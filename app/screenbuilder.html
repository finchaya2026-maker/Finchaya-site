Everyone's requests (administrators)

A screen lists the stocks that match what you asked for; it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything, and FinChaya is not a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. Technical readings use end-of-day prices (a week or month still in progress counts as it stands); chart patterns, fund activity and shareholding are the same readings as on the Screener page; ratios are worked out from the company's filed results at the latest price. A reading that cannot be made for a stock leaves that stock out and is counted, so a screen never looks cleaner than the data behind it. “Industry average” is the median of the companies in the same industry that have the ratio, when at least five do.

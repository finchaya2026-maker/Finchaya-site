The cost of a quiet underperformer

Most funds lose to their benchmark. Few investors notice in time.

A gap of two or three percentage points a year sounds survivable. Over a twenty-year SIP it decides whether you retire on one corpus or another. The damage is invisible month to month, which is exactly why it persists.

How it works

Three steps.

Nothing here is opinion. Every number traces back to a disclosed holding or a published price.

STEP 01

Read the portfolio

SEBI requires every AMC to disclose full holdings monthly. We parse those files directly — every stock, every weight, straight from the source.

STEP 02

Look through

See which companies and sectors sit underneath a fund, and how much of each — and, across several funds, where they quietly hold the same things.

STEP 03

Set it beside the returns

Returns against the fund's own benchmark and against other funds in its category, over the periods that matter — measured, not predicted.

Mixing daily, weekly and monthly candles
  • Each side of a condition has an on choice: Daily, Weekly or Monthly. You can mix them, for example a daily 5 EMA crossing a daily 50 EMA AND a monthly MACD above its value a month ago.
  • For weekly and monthly there is a second choice, uses: Last finished candle reads only the last completed week or month. It is steady, but it can be up to a week or a month old, so it can miss a turn that has already begun. Candle in progress reads this week or month as it stands at each day’s close, so it is fresher, but it can change from day to day and flip back before the week or month ends.
  • Neither looks ahead: a finished candle only counts from the day it finishes, and the one in progress uses only the days so far. Counting back (“weeks ago”, “months ago”) goes in candles of that size: with in progress, 0 is the current one and 1 the last finished one; with finished, 0 is the last finished one and 1 the one before.
  • Trades are still made on daily candles, and stops, targets and holding limits work on daily candles.

Stepped stop (raises the stop-loss above as the price rises)

Example: stop-loss 5, rises 10, moves up 5. The stop starts 5% below your entry. Once the price is up 10% it moves to your entry price (no loss), at +20% it is 5% above your entry, at +30% 10% above, and so on. It only ever moves up. This needs a stop-loss to start from. It is different from the follow-the-high stop, which always sits a fixed distance below the highest price reached. If you use both, whichever is closer to the price applies.

A back-test shows what a set of rules would have done in the past, on the prices we hold. It does not predict what they will do next, and it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Results ignore tax, impact cost and dividends, assume every order is filled at the price shown, and cover only stocks listed today, which flatters any rule tested across many stocks.
